In a world where being a mom, wife, daughter, sister, and all of the above comes before everything else.. I have discovered the secret to domestic harmony. 

I find complete joy and happiness in the lines of code. The challenge of making bits and pieces of code do what I want it to do. 

I am not endlessly tinkering with Obsidian, CSS, HTML, and Javascript.. I am performing preventive domestic harmony maintenance. Blood, sweat, and tears for the continued stability of my household. 

So if you have bad news for me.... ask if I am mid project first :)
